From POC to production

How the advisor would sit inside the live Ahloki store.

  1. Ahloki websiteAdvisor embedded on shop, collection and product pages
  2. AI Scent & Wellness AdvisorConversation, memory and gift mode
  3. AI / recommendation engineLanguage model for intent, scoring for relevance
  4. Live product catalogueNotes, pricing and stock from the ecommerce platform
  5. Product recommendationTop matches with transparent reasons
  6. Ahloki product page / basketStraight into the existing checkout

The production version can connect the AI assistant to Ahloki's live product catalogue, pricing, stock availability and ecommerce platform. This demo runs entirely in the browser with a local catalogue and needs no API key.
